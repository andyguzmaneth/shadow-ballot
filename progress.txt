## Codebase Patterns
- @noble/secp256k1 v3 API: `Point` (not `ProjectivePoint`), `etc.mod()`, `etc.invert()`, `hashes.sha256` (not `etc.sha256Sync`)
- @noble/hashes v2 exports require `.js` suffix: `@noble/hashes/sha3.js`, `@noble/hashes/utils.js`
- noble/secp256k1 v3 `verify()` always hashes msg with sha256 internally — cannot use it for keccak256-hashed messages. Use manual ECDSA verify instead.
- Curve order N hardcoded: `0xFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFEBAAEDCE6AF48A03BBFD25E8CD0364141n`
- Must initialize noble hashes before use: `hashes.sha256 = ...` and `hashes.hmacSha256 = ...`
